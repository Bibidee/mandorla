# Mandorla

**When two truths overlap, Mandorla finds the fair middle.**

Mandorla is a shared-decision protocol built on [GenLayer](https://genlayer.com) for disputes and reviews that shouldn't be reduced to a binary yes-or-no. Instead of a single arbiter picking a winner, both sides submit their account and supporting evidence, and GenLayer's validator network reasons over the full record to produce a proportional outcome — a split payout, partial credit, shared fault, a conditional approval, a staged release, or an honest admission that the evidence is insufficient to decide.

Every case lives on-chain as a GenLayer Intelligent Contract (`MandorlaSharedDecision`). A claimant opens a case, a respondent adds their side, both parties submit evidence, and either party can request resolution once the record is complete. The verdict — including a written rationale, confidence score, and settlement instruction — is generated by non-deterministic validator consensus and stored immutably.

## How it works

1. **Open a case** — describe the agreement, your position, and the outcome you're requesting.
2. **Respond** — the other party adds their account and a counter-outcome.
3. **Submit evidence** — either side (or a neutral party) attaches evidence tiles: documents, message threads, timelines, expert notes.
4. **Advance to ready** — once the record is complete, either party marks the case ready.
5. **Request resolution** — GenLayer validators weigh both truths and the evidence to compute a proportional middle outcome.
6. **Settlement** — the final result includes a payout split, responsibility split, confidence score, and reasoning.

## Stack

- **Frontend** — Next.js (App Router), deployed on Vercel
- **Contract** — GenLayer Intelligent Contract (`contract/mandorla_shared_decision.py`), Studionet
- **Wallet** — injected wallet (MetaMask, Rabby) via `eth_sendTransaction`, no WalletConnect
- **Chain calls** — [`genlayer-js`](https://www.npmjs.com/package/genlayer-js) for calldata encoding and reads; writes are wrapped through the GenLayer consensus contract's `addTransaction`

## Project layout

- `src/` — the Next.js app (Observatory, Resolve console, Settlement ledger, case detail)
- `contract/` — the GenLayer contract, deploy script, and test/smoke-test scripts

See [`contract/README.md`](contract/README.md) for contract setup, deployment, and method reference.
