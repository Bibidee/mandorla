# Mandorla — More Information

Mandorla is a GenLayer shared-decision protocol for disputes where the fairest result may be proportional rather than binary.

## Evidence-backed verdicts

Each evidence submission is tied to the submitting party's wallet and includes an HTTPS source URL plus its SHA-256 digest. During resolution, the leader and validators independently fetch the source, reject altered content, and derive the outcome from the verified material. A verdict must cite its source-backed findings in `supported_facts`.

## Enforceable settlement

Cases use native GEN escrow. The parties fund the exact amount at stake before requesting resolution. The contract derives payout amounts from the consensus basis-point split and releases escrow through `execute_settlement`. Instructions generated by a model are descriptive only; they cannot alter transfers.

## Contract lifecycle

1. Create a GEN case.
2. Respond to the case.
3. Submit HTTPS evidence with SHA-256 commitments.
4. Fund the exact GEN amount at stake.
5. Advance to ready and request resolution.
6. Execute the authorized settlement, or retain escrow for a manual/no-evidence result.
